Frequently asked questions

Where is my game saved?
On your device. The game saves automatically every month of game time and when you leave the app. Deleting the app deletes the save.

Can I start over?
Yes. Tap the house button in the top right corner and start a new company.

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Crude Empire works fully offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No.

How do I change the language?
Open the menu with the house button and pick Settings. The game is available in 17 languages.

My pumps keep breaking down.
Hire enough field workers, keep pumps at 100% speed or less, and turn on automatic repair under Business → Strategies.
