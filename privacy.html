Privacy Policy

Crude Empire · Last updated 27 October 2026

In short

Crude Empire does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Your saved game and your settings (such as the language you picked) are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

The game works fully offline. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services. It does not ask for an account, your location, your contacts, your photos or any other personal information.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, the game is safe to play at any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
